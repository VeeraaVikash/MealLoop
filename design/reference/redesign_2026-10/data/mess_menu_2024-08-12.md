1. **Sample calendar:** this board starts Monday 12 Aug 2024, so the whole demo should use that week: Mon 12 Aug to Sun 18 Aug, with Wed 14 Aug as "today". It matches the existing mess samples (Wed 14 Aug, the 5 to 11 Aug report). The Outing module's October dates should move to the same week.
2. **Snacks** are the fourth slot. Open question: do students answer intent or scan an entry QR for snacks? Draft: Snacks are shown for information only.
3. **Tiers:** Highlights ([H]), Mains (breads, rice, curries, dal), Comes with it (sambar, rasam, pickle, fryums, salad, milk, banana, tea and coffee).
4. **Non-veg** items carry a non-veg mark. Gravy lines marked `**` are non-veg options (Fish, Mutton and Chicken). The board does not say who they are for (VERIFY).
5. **"or" and "/" items** are listed as separate dishes, with no "or" shown and no choose-one UI (owner decision). The old optional curry-preference question is removed.
6. **Alternate-Wednesday special** (Chicken or Paneer Briyani) is a ticket on the Wednesday Lunch tab, tied to the Special pass. "Special items are limited" is shown as a Limited tag.
7. **Feedback dishes** come from the selected meal's list (8 to 14 items), not a fixed five. The picker shows Highlights and Mains first, with a "More dishes" expander.
8. **Admin Menu** must handle four meals, long lists, highlight flags and non-veg flags. Calories and nutrition stay out.
